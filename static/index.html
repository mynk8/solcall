Policy Primer

How Delhi's Solar Subsidy Really Works

⚠️ The Gross-vs-Net Rule (AMEND2 cl. 4.14): This is the #1 misunderstanding for Delhi households. Your power subsidy bracket is decided by GROSS consumption (before solar), but it is applied to reduce your NET bill (after solar).

1. Gross ≤ 200 Units/Month:
Qualifies for 100% subsidy. With solar net metering, your net electricity bill is ₹0, plus you earn the under-200 incentive of ₹1.50 per unit self-consumed!

2. Gross 201–400 Units/Month:
Qualifies for a 50% power subsidy capped at ₹800. Solar reduces your units from the top slab (₹4.50/unit) down to ₹0.

3. Gross > 400 Units/Month:
Zero power subsidy! But solar provides its highest financial savings here because it offsets Delhi's progressive ₹6.50–₹8.00/unit slabs.

4. Central + Delhi Dual Capital Subsidy:
On a 3 kW system, you receive ₹78,000 from PM Surya Ghar (Central) PLUS up to ₹78,000 from Delhi State Policy (₹26k/kW), yielding up to ₹1,56,000 in total capital subsidies!
